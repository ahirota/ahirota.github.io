# Pottery Barn Teen
My main role at WSI was doing front end development for the Pottery Barn Teen brand. Anything that dealt with the actual content of the site was under our purview. We utilized `HTML`, `CSS`, `Javascript`, and `Vue.js` to accomplish our tasks. We also worked extensively with Adobe Experience Manager as a CMS for some of our dynamic content on the site. 

## The Regular Routine
To give a sense of our process, we used JIRA and Agile practices in collaboration with our cross functional teams to push changes to the site on a weekly basis. 

1. Planning stage: The Creative and Site Management teams would define direction on what needed to be built, updated, changed, etc...
   1. Occasionally we'd need to assert what was feasible with the constraints of working our systems.
2. Working stage: Once direction was passed to us we would leverage our skills and do the work.
3. Testing/Feedback stage: Once we believed to have satisfied requirements, we would puysh our changes into a testing environment and ask stakeholders to review the changes.
   1. Sometimes during testing and review, comments and callouts would arise and we would continuously iterate until our stakeholders were satisfied.
4. Deployment stage: Assuming everything went well, we would bundle that change in with a weekly deployment build and push to production.

If it seems like PDCA with a few extra steps, that's because it is. Here's a small diagram detailing this. Oftentimes we worked on multiple tickets in parallel in order to meet tight deadlines.
